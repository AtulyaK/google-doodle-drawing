Field Note 01

Lantern Ink Atelier

A quiet study of an alchemy lesson: inspect a luminous stencil, trace each component, and watch a small invented object answer the finished mark.

This is an original, mood-inspired project. It is not official Witch Hat Atelier artwork, and it does not use copied characters, symbols, panels, game assets, or story material.

Two moments from the lesson

From stencil to response

Plate I

The Moonwell stencil

A broad vessel, three gathering rays, and a suspended seed form a new three-step practice mark. Dashed paths suggest order without demanding perfect tracing.

  • Ghosted guides identify the current stroke.
  • Numbered anchors communicate sequence without color alone.
  • Warm ink records the learner's own line over the cool stencil.

Plate II

The lantern bloom answers

Completion is shown as a gentle transformation rather than a score burst: folded paper opens, a new sprout rises, and the traced mark remains visible beneath the result.

  • The response is readable without motion or sound.
  • The object and sigil are newly authored for this study.
  • Paper, ink, and light carry the handmade mood.

Design boundary

Inspired by a feeling, not an adaptation

The visual direction explores generic ideas of careful craft, illustrated fantasy, apprenticeship, and discovery. All SVG shapes, compositions, names, copy, and color choices in this subproject were created for this repository. The page is static, works offline once downloaded, and loads no external images, scripts, fonts, or services.
